# MONK

MONK is a quiet daily practice of prayer and Scripture, set out as one book. This is MONK v5.5 with sample content.

**Open it:** https://keithatergatha.github.io/monk-demo/

- **Today**: the hour, a psalm verse for it, and the table of what is set for the day.
- **Movements**: the morning Offering (four movements) and the evening Examen (five). The Examen's review asks three questions that change each evening, one of them about the day itself.
- **Word**: a devotional on the day's passage, and a reading plan through the whole Bible in fifteen-minute sittings.
- **Prayer**: a prayer list where each person or thing has its own page, what you are praying for now, your notes, and a suggested focus. Also guided prayers, prayed one part at a time: St Patrick's Breastplate (Kuno Meyer, 1911) and three of Cranmer's collects (Book of Common Prayer, 1662).
- **Record**: Walk, which shows where you have been, described and never graded.

Every person and every prayer here is invented. The sample day is always today. Nothing is sent anywhere: what you tap and write stays in your own browser. Scripture is from the World English Bible (public domain).

This is a static site served by GitHub Pages.
